**Caption:** A full-scale replica of Sputnik 1 on display at the National Air and Space Museum, Washington D.C. The original Soviet satellite, launched 4 October 1957, was 58 cm in diameter and weighed 83.6 kg. Its four trailing antennae broadcast a distinctive radio beep audible worldwide, marking humanity's first artificial Earth satellite.
