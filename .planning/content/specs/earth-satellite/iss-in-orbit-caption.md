**Caption:** The International Space Station (ISS) photographed from Space Shuttle Atlantis during the STS-132 final flyaround on 23 May 2010. At approximately 420 tonnes and 109 m in length, the ISS is the largest artificial satellite in Earth orbit, circling at ~400 km altitude every 92 minutes and hosting continuous human habitation since November 2000.
