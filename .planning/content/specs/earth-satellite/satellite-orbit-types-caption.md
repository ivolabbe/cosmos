**Caption:** Scale comparison of the major Earth satellite orbit families: Low Earth Orbit (LEO, ~160–2,000 km), Medium Earth Orbit (MEO, ~2,000–35,500 km including GPS at ~20,200 km), and Geostationary Orbit (GEO, 35,786 km). The diagram illustrates why different mission types occupy different orbital regimes — from the ISS and Starlink mega-constellations in LEO to GPS navigation satellites in MEO and telecommunications satellites in GEO.
