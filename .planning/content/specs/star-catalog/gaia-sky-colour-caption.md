**Caption:** Gaia's all-sky view of the Milky Way and neighboring galaxies, built from brightness and color measurements of nearly 1.7 billion stars observed by the ESA Gaia satellite between July 2014 and May 2016. The bright band of the Galactic plane dominates the image; dark lanes mark interstellar dust clouds. The Large and Small Magellanic Clouds are visible at lower right.
