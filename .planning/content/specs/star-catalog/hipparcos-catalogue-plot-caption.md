**Caption:** Equirectangular all-sky plot of stars brighter than apparent magnitude 5 from the Hipparcos Catalogue, with right ascension on the horizontal axis and declination on the vertical axis. Stars are color-coded by spectral type and scaled by brightness. Constellation boundaries and the ecliptic are overlaid.
