**Caption:** All-sky map of approximately 2.4 million stars from the Tycho and Hipparcos catalogs, rendered in plate carrée projection with a visual magnitude threshold of 3.0. Star sizes correspond to relative brightness. The Milky Way is visible as the faint band of dense star field across the center of the image. Data were collected by the ESA Hipparcos satellite between November 1989 and March 1993.
