**Caption:** TESS satellite light curve of Mimosa (Beta Crucis), one of the brightest Beta Cephei variable stars and a prominent star in the Southern Cross (Crux). Mimosa is historically significant as the first star of any type to have its pulsation modes identified through polarimetric asteroseismology (2021). The multiperiodic pulsation visible in this light curve reflects simultaneously excited pressure modes of slightly different frequencies, creating a characteristic beating pattern.
