**Caption:** Digitized Sky Survey image of Spica (Alpha Virginis), the 16th brightest star in the night sky at apparent magnitude 0.97 and one of the most prominent Beta Cephei variables. Spica is a binary system whose primary component is a hot B1 III–IV star (effective temperature ~25,300 K) that pulsates as a Beta Cephei variable. The star is notable for an apparent cessation of pulsation observed around 1970. Image created using Aladin Sky Atlas and Digitized Sky Survey data.
