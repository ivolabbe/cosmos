**Caption:** TESS satellite light curve of Beta Cephei (Alfirk), the prototype Beta Cephei variable star. The rapid, small-amplitude brightness oscillation repeats every 4 hours 34 minutes (the star's dominant pulsation period) with an amplitude of approximately 0.11 magnitudes. This short-period variation is driven by the iron opacity (kappa) mechanism deep in the stellar interior. Beta Cephei is a B1 IV subgiant located about 690 light-years away in the constellation Cepheus.
