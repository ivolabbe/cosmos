**Caption:** TESS satellite light curve of Spica (Alpha Virginis), magnitude 0.97 and one of the 20 brightest stars in the sky, showing its Beta Cephei pulsation behaviour. Spica is a spectroscopic binary whose primary component pulsates with periods characteristic of the Beta Cephei class. The star is scientifically notable for an apparent cessation of pulsation reported around 1970, making it one of the most intriguing examples of the class.
