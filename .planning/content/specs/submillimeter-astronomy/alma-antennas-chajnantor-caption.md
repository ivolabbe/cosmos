**Caption:** Antennas of the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) on the Chajnantor plateau at 5,000 metres altitude in the Chilean Atacama Desert. ALMA's 66 antennas operate at millimeter and submillimeter wavelengths, making it the most powerful ground-based telescope in this regime.
