**Caption:** The James Clerk Maxwell Telescope (JCMT, centre) on Mauna Kea, Hawaii, flanked by the Caltech Submillimeter Observatory (left) and the Submillimeter Array (right). The JCMT's 15-metre dish makes it the world's largest single-dish submillimeter telescope; it first operated in 1987 and remains active today.
