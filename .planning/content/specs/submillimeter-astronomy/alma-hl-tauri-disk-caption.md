**Caption:** ALMA image of the protoplanetary disk surrounding the young star HL Tauri, located about 450 light-years away. The dark and bright rings reveal gaps likely carved by forming planets. This 2014 image was the sharpest ever produced by ALMA and demonstrated the power of submillimeter interferometry for studying planet formation.
