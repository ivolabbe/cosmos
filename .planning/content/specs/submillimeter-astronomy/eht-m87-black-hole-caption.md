**Caption:** The first direct image of a black hole: the supermassive black hole at the centre of galaxy Messier 87, imaged by the Event Horizon Telescope at a wavelength of 1.3 mm. The bright ring is emission from hot plasma near the event horizon; the dark region is the black hole's shadow. Released in April 2019.
**Credit:** EHT Collaboration

**Why:** Spectacular and relevant science result, but the image is very abstract (blurry orange ring) and does not directly illustrate the concept of submillimeter astronomy as a technique. Better as a supporting example than a lead image.
