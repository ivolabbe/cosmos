# Image Caption: artemis-2-earthset.jpg

## Caption
Earth sets behind the Moon's cratered limb, photographed by the Artemis II crew on 6 April 2026 as Orion flew around the Moon — the first crewed cislunar voyage since Apollo 17 in 1972. The foreground shows Ohm crater. The image echoes the iconic Apollo 8 Earthrise photograph taken 58 years earlier.
