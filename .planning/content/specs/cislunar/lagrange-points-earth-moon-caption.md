# Image Caption: lagrange-points-earth-moon.jpg

## Caption
Contour plot of the effective gravitational potential in the Earth-Moon system, showing all five Lagrange points (L1–L5). The saddle points L1, L2, and L3 lie along the Earth-Moon line and are dynamically unstable; L4 and L5, located at the tips of equilateral triangles with Earth and Moon, are stable.
