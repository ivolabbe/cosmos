# Image Caption: nrho-infographic.jpg

## Caption
NASA infographic explaining the Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit (NRHO) chosen for the Artemis Gateway station. The graphic shows the orbit's extreme ellipticity — skimming within 1,500 km of the north lunar pole and reaching 70,000 km over the south — and highlights advantages including near-continuous Earth contact and efficient fuel budgets for lunar surface access.
