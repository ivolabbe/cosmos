# Image Caption: nrho-diagram.png

## Caption
A Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit (NRHO) in the Earth-Moon system. The highly elongated, seven-day polar orbit loops from roughly 1,500 km above one lunar pole to ~70,000 km above the other — selected for NASA's Gateway station because of its stable station-keeping, continuous Earth communications, and efficient access to the lunar surface.
