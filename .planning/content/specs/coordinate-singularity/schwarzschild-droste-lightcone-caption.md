**Caption:** Light cone diagram in standard Schwarzschild–Droste coordinates showing light rays near a black hole. The coordinate singularity at the Schwarzschild radius r = r_s is clearly visible: light cones degenerate and the coordinate time axis stretches to infinity at the horizon, even though the spacetime geometry is perfectly regular there.
