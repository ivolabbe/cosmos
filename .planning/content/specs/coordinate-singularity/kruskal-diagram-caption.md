**Caption:** Kruskal–Szekeres diagram of the maximally extended Schwarzschild spacetime, showing the Schwarzschild coordinate chart overlaid with light cones and the worldline of an infalling particle. The event horizon (r = r_s) appears as a regular boundary in these coordinates, demonstrating that the apparent singularity in Schwarzschild coordinates is a coordinate artifact. The true physical singularity at r = 0 appears as the hyperbolic boundary at the top.
