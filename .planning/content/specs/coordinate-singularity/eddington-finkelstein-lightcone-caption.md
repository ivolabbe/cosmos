**Caption:** Light cone diagram in Eddington–Finkelstein coordinates showing radially ingoing and outgoing light rays near a Schwarzschild black hole. Unlike Schwarzschild coordinates, the metric is regular at the event horizon (r = r_s); light cones tilt smoothly inward as r decreases, clearly displaying the one-way nature of the horizon.
