**Caption:** Spherical polar coordinate system (r, θ, φ) showing colatitude θ measured from the north pole, longitude φ, and radius ρ. At the poles (θ = 0° or θ = 180°), the azimuthal angle φ is undefined — a classic everyday example of a coordinate singularity. The surface itself is smooth; only the coordinate description breaks down.
