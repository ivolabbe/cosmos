**Caption:** Diagram illustrating Kepler's three laws of planetary motion. An elliptical orbit is shown with the Sun at one focus; the major axis connects perihelion and aphelion. Equal areas (A₁ = A₂) are swept in equal times (Kepler's Second Law), and the relationship T² ∝ a³ (Third Law) is indicated.
