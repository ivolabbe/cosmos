**Caption:** The Hubble classification scheme ("tuning fork diagram") showing galaxy morphology types. Elliptical galaxies (E0–E7, left) are classified by their axis ratio b/a, where E0 appears nearly circular and E7 is most elongated. The major axis is the reference diameter used to define the ellipticity parameter En = 10(1 − b/a).
