**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope composite image of the Milky Way's central bulge, showing approximately 26,000 light-years of densely packed stars. The region is dominated by metal-rich red giant stars alongside younger Sun-like white stars. Studies of this field reveal that stars richer in heavy elements orbit the galactic centre faster and with less disordered motion than their metal-poor counterparts — a direct observational consequence of different formation histories.
