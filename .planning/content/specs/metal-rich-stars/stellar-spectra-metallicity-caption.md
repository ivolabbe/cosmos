**Caption:** Comparison of stellar absorption spectra at decreasing metallicity, from the Sun (top) through the low-iron star CD −38 245, the extremely iron-poor star HE 0107-5240, and a theoretical Population III star with zero metals (bottom). The progressive fading of iron (Fe) and nickel (Ni) absorption lines illustrates the [Fe/H] scale directly: the Sun harbours one iron atom per 31,000 hydrogen atoms, while HE 0107-5240 has only one per 6.8 billion.
