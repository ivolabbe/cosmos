**Caption:** The Hyades open star cluster, the nearest star cluster to the Sun at approximately 150 light-years. With a metallicity of [Fe/H] = +0.14, the Hyades is mildly super-solar and has historically served as the reference benchmark for "metal-rich" stars in professional astronomical glossaries. The bright reddish star at upper right is Aldebaran, which is a foreground star unrelated to the cluster.
