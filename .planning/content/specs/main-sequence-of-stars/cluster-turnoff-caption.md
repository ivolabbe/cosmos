**Caption:** H-R diagrams for two open clusters of different ages, illustrating the main sequence turn-off point. In the older cluster (right), more massive stars have already left the main sequence and evolved into giants, shifting the turn-off to lower luminosities. The turn-off luminosity directly yields the cluster age via isochrone fitting.
