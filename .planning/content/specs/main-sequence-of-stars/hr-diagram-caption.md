**Caption:** Hertzsprung–Russell diagram plotting luminosity against surface temperature (colour) for nearby stars from the Hipparcos Catalogue. The prominent diagonal band running from the upper-left (hot, luminous O stars) to the lower-right (cool, dim M dwarfs) is the main sequence. Giants, supergiants, and white dwarfs occupy distinct regions off the main sequence.
