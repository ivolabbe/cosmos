**Caption:** The near side of the Moon showing the prominent dark maria — ancient basaltic lava plains — contrasting with the brighter, heavily cratered highlands. The large dark regions visible to the naked eye gave rise to the phrase "the man in the Moon." Major features include Mare Imbrium (upper left), Mare Tranquillitatis (centre right), Mare Serenitatis (upper right), and Oceanus Procellarum (left).
