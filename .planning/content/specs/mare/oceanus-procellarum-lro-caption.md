**Caption:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) image of Oceanus Procellarum ("Ocean of Storms"), the largest mare on the Moon at roughly 4 million km². The smooth, dark basaltic surface is pocked by younger impact craters, with subtle wrinkle ridges visible — compressional features formed as ancient lava flows cooled and contracted. GRAIL gravity data revealed ancient rectangular rift structures beneath this surface, suggesting the plain formed partly through tectonic rifting rather than a single giant impact.
