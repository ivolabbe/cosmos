**Caption:** Animated comparison of a star imaged without adaptive optics (left, blurred by atmospheric jitter) and with adaptive optics correction (right, sharp). The fast tip-tilt mirror in an AO system nulls the dominant low-order pointing jitter, recovering close to the diffraction-limited point spread function. This is the primary mitigation technique for atmospheric jitter at ground-based observatories.
