**Caption:** A laser guide star beam fired from ESO's Very Large Telescope at Cerro Paranal, Chile, toward the centre of the Milky Way. The artificial laser star provides a wavefront reference for the adaptive optics system, enabling the fast steering mirror to measure and correct atmospheric jitter in real time. The natural seeing blurs starlight on timescales of ~10–30 ms; AO corrects this at rates of hundreds to thousands of Hz.
