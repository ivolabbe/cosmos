**Caption:** Animation showing how an orbiting exoplanet causes its host star to wobble around the common centre of mass, producing a periodic Doppler shift in the star's spectrum. In planet-search spectroscopy, stellar surface phenomena (spots, granulation, oscillations) produce similar but astrophysically-driven apparent Doppler shifts — this "radial velocity jitter" is a major systematic noise source that can mimic or mask planetary signals.
