**Caption:** Diagram of the classic P Cygni spectral line profile, showing the characteristic blueshifted absorption trough to the left of the rest wavelength and the broad emission peak to the right. The absorption arises from circumstellar material expanding toward the observer; the emission arises from the dense stellar wind surrounding the star.
