**Caption:** Historical light curve of P Cygni showing its brightness variations from the 17th century to the present. The main plot uses AAVSO photometric data; the inset is adapted from de Groot (1988) in the Irish Astronomical Journal and shows the star's dramatic outbursts in the 1600s before it settled near magnitude 4.8.
