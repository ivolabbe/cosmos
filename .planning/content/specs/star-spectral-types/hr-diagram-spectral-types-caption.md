**Caption:** Hertzsprung-Russell diagram plotting luminosity against colour (temperature) for approximately 22,000 stars from the Hipparcos Catalogue. Spectral types run along the horizontal axis from hot blue O-type stars (left) to cool red M-type stars (right), showing how spectral classification relates to stellar evolution and the main sequence.
