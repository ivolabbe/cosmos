JunoCam image of Jupiter processed by citizen scientists Gerald Eichstädt and Seán Doran from raw data obtained during Juno's 12th close flyby on 1 April 2018. The image shows Jupiter's swirling cloud bands and atmospheric turbulence — features that Juno's Microwave Radiometer revealed extend hundreds of kilometres below the visible cloud tops. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS/Gerald Eichstädt/Seán Doran (PIA21985, public domain).
