Enhanced-colour JunoCam image of Jupiter's Great Red Spot, captured on 10 July 2017 during Juno's seventh close flyby (Perijove 7) from approximately 13,917 kilometres above the cloud tops. Juno's gravity science instrument subsequently determined the storm extends approximately 500 kilometres below the cloud tops. Image processed by citizen scientist Jason Major from raw JunoCam data. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS/Jason Major (PIA21772, public domain).
