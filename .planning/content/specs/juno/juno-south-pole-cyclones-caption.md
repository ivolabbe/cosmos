Annotated composite image depicting the movement of circumpolar cyclones at Jupiter's south pole between 2016 and 2021, as observed by Juno's JIRAM instrument. The south pole hosts five cyclones arranged in a pentagonal pattern around a central storm. Tracking these cyclones over five years revealed that they slowly drift pole-ward through a process called beta drift. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/ASI/INAF/JIRAM (PIA24967, public domain).
