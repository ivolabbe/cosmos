**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of the Southern Crab Nebula (He2-104 / Hen 2-104) in Centaurus, a D-type symbiotic star system several thousand light-years away. The hourglass-shaped bipolar nebula was sculpted by mass loss from a Mira-type red giant orbited by a white dwarf; the two nested hourglass lobes record successive ejection episodes. This image was taken in March 2019 to mark the 29th anniversary of the Hubble Space Telescope.
