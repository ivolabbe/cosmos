**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope composite image of R Aquarii, the nearest known symbiotic binary star (~711 light-years away), compiled from observations spanning 2014 to 2023. The image shows the interacting binary at centre — a Mira-type red giant and its white dwarf companion — surrounded by a dynamic nebula of plasma jets and spiral filaments ejected during the white dwarf's explosive outbursts. The jets extend roughly 248 billion miles (24 times the diameter of our solar system) and travel at over one million miles per hour.
