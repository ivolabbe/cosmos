**Caption:** Artist's impression of the R Aquarii symbiotic binary system, showing the cool Mira-type red giant (right) transferring material through a stellar wind to its white dwarf companion (left), which is surrounded by an accretion disk. A jet of ionised gas is launched perpendicular to the disk.
