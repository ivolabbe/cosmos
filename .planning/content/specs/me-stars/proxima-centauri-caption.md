**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of Proxima Centauri, the nearest star to the Sun at 4.24 light-years. Classified as M5.5Ve, Proxima Centauri is one of the most studied dMe stars — a red dwarf with hydrogen emission lines driven by intense chromospheric magnetic activity. It is also a UV Ceti-type flare star.
