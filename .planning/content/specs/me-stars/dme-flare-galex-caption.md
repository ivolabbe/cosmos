**Caption:** NASA Galaxy Evolution Explorer (GALEX) ultraviolet observation of a record stellar flare from the dMe red dwarf GJ 3685A, observed 24 April 2004. The star erupted twice in rapid succession, releasing roughly one million times more energy than a typical solar flare — illustrating the extreme UV and optical emission that characterises active dMe (Me-type dwarf) flare stars.
