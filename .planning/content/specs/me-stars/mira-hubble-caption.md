**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope (Faint Object Camera) image of Mira A (right, spectral type M7 IIIe) and its hot compact companion (left), separated by only 0.6 arcseconds. Mira is the prototype long-period variable Mira star and the canonical example of a Me giant — its emission lines arise from shock waves propagating through its pulsating atmosphere near maximum light.
