# Caption: jewel-box-wide-eso.jpg

**Caption:** Wide-field view of the Jewel Box cluster (NGC 4755) in Crux, captured with the Wide Field Imager on the MPG/ESO 2.2-metre telescope at La Silla Observatory. The image covers roughly 20 arcminutes and shows the cluster embedded in the rich stellar fields of the Galactic plane. Kappa Crucis, a B3 Ia blue supergiant and the cluster's namesake, is among the brightest stars. This image was combined using B, V, and infrared I filters.
