# Caption: achernar-shape.jpg

**Caption:** Interferometric profile of Achernar (Alpha Eridani), the brightest Be star, derived from ~20,000 VLTI interferograms taken between September and November 2002. The fitted ellipse reveals an axis ratio of 1.56 ± 0.05 — an equatorial radius 56% larger than the polar radius — making Achernar the most oblate star measured at that time. This extreme flattening results from rotation at ~250 km/s, close to the critical velocity. Angular semi-major axis is 0.00253 arcsec.
