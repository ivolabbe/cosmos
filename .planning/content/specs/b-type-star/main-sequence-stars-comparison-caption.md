# Caption: main-sequence-stars-comparison.jpg

**Caption:** Size comparison of main-sequence stars rendered in Blender, from left to right: Proxima Centauri (red M-dwarf), the Sun (yellow G-dwarf), Sirius (white A-dwarf), and Spica (blue B1 III-IV giant). Spica's radius of ~7.5 solar radii and surface temperature of ~25,300 K illustrate how much larger and hotter B-type stars are compared to solar-type stars.
