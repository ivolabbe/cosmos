# Caption: jewel-box-cluster.jpg

**Caption:** The Jewel Box (NGC 4755), a young open cluster in the constellation Crux, imaged by the NASA/ESA Hubble Space Telescope. Several pale blue B-type supergiant stars dominate the scene alongside a solitary ruby-red supergiant. The brightest blue members are massive stars of spectral type B0–B5, with ages of only ~14 million years — a blink in cosmic time. The colour contrast vividly illustrates the difference in temperature between hot blue stars and cooler evolved supergiants.
