**Caption:** Decay scheme of potassium-40 (⁴⁰K), showing its two competing decay modes: beta-minus decay to calcium-40 (⁴⁰Ca, ~89% of decays) and electron capture to an excited state of argon-40 (⁴⁰Ar, ~11% of decays) followed by gamma-ray emission at 1.460 MeV. The accumulation of ⁴⁰Ar from K capture is the basis of potassium-argon geochronology, used to date rocks over millions to billions of years.
