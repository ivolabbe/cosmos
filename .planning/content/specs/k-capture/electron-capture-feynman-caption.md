**Caption:** Leading-order Feynman diagrams for electron capture decay, showing the weak-force interaction at the quark level. An up quark (u) in the proton emits a W⁺ boson which is absorbed by the incoming electron (e⁻), converting the up quark to a down quark (turning the proton into a neutron) and producing an electron neutrino (νₑ). This is the fundamental process underlying K capture.
