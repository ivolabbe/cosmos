**Caption:** Diagram of the two-stage atomic process following K-electron capture. Left: the nucleus (centre) absorbs an inner K-shell electron, creating a vacancy. Right: an outer-shell electron (L or M shell) drops in to fill the K-shell vacancy, releasing the energy difference as a characteristic X-ray photon (or alternatively ejecting an Auger electron). The daughter element is identified by detecting these characteristic X-rays.
