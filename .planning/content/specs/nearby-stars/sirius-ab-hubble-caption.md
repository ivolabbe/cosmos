**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of Sirius A and its white dwarf companion Sirius B (arrowed, lower left). Sirius A is the brightest star in the night sky at apparent magnitude −1.46 and lies just 8.71 light-years from Earth. Sirius B, though slightly less massive than Sirius A, has already completed stellar evolution and collapsed into a white dwarf the size of Earth.
