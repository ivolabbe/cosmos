**Caption:** Proxima Centauri, the closest star to the Sun at 4.25 light-years, imaged by the Hubble Space Telescope. Despite Hubble's resolving power, the star appears as a bright point of light — a testament to the vast distances between even the nearest stars. Proxima Centauri is a dim red dwarf and flare star in the constellation Centaurus.
