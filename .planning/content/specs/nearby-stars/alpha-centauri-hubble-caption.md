**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of Alpha Centauri A (left) and Alpha Centauri B (right), the two main stars of the nearest stellar system to Earth at 4.3 light-years. Alpha Cen A (G2V, Sun-like) and Alpha Cen B (K1V, orange dwarf) orbit each other every 79 years. A third member, the red dwarf Proxima Centauri, lies about 0.2 light-years further away.
