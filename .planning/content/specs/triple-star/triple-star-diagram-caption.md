**Caption:** Schematic diagram of a hierarchical triple star system. The inner binary pair orbits their shared centre of mass; the outer (third) star orbits the inner binary at a much greater distance. This nested orbital arrangement is the configuration that gives triple systems long-term dynamical stability.
