**Caption:** Diagram of the KOI-5 triple star system, showing the three gravitationally bound stars (A, B, and C) and the orbit of planet KOI-5Ab around the primary star. Stars A and B orbit each other every 30 years; star C orbits the AB pair every 400 years. The planet's orbital plane is tilted 50° relative to the AB orbital plane.
