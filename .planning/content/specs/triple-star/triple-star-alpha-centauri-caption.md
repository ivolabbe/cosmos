**Caption:** Composite optical and X-ray image of the Alpha Centauri system — the nearest triple star system to the Sun, at 4.24–4.34 light-years. The bright pair (Alpha Centauri A and B) forms a close binary with an orbital period of 79 years; the third member, Proxima Centauri (not visible here), orbits at roughly 13,000 AU.
