**Caption:** The Sirius binary system imaged by the Hubble Space Telescope. Sirius A (centre, overexposed) is the brightest star in the night sky and a classic Am (metallic-line) chemically peculiar star (CP1 class); its faint companion Sirius B, a white dwarf, appears at lower left. Diffraction spikes and rings are optical artefacts of the telescope.
