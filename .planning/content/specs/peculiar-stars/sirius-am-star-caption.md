**Caption:** The Sirius binary system imaged by the Hubble Space Telescope. Sirius A (overexposed bright star, centre) is the brightest star in the night sky and a well-known Am (metallic-line) chemically peculiar star (CP1 class); its white dwarf companion Sirius B appears at lower left. The spikes and rings are optical artefacts of the telescope.
