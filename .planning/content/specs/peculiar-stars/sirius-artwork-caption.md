**Caption:** Artist's impression of Sirius A (large, blue-white star) and its companion Sirius B (small, hot white dwarf at left), as seen from outside the binary system. The two stars orbit each other every 50 years. Sirius A is classified as a metallic-line Am star (CP1 chemically peculiar star).
