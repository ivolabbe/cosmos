**Caption:** R Leporis imaged by ALMA at the highest angular resolution ever achieved by the telescope (5 milli-arcseconds) — equivalent to resolving a 10-metre bus on the Moon. Orange shows submillimetre emission from the stellar surface; blue traces hydrogen cyanide (HCN) maser emission revealing a ring of gas surrounding the dying star. The image illustrates the powerful stellar wind and mass-loss that characterise N-type (C-N) carbon stars on the asymptotic giant branch.
