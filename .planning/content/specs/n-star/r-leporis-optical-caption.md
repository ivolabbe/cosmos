**Caption:** R Leporis (Hind's Crimson Star), the archetypal N-type carbon star, photographed through an 85mm lens. The star's intense red colour — caused by carbon molecules in its atmosphere filtering out blue light — is strikingly apparent against the surrounding field stars. R Leporis is a Mira-type variable located about 1,490 light-years away in the constellation Lepus.
