**Caption:** ALMA image of the protoplanetary disk around V883 Orionis, a young FU Orionis-type eruptive variable star in Orion. The disk structure is revealed at millimetre wavelengths; this object underwent a luminosity outburst caused by a sudden increase in mass accretion — the same mechanism responsible for FU Ori-class nebular variable outbursts.
