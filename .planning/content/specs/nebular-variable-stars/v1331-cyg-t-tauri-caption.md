**Caption:** V1331 Cygni, a T Tauri-type nebular variable star in the dark cloud Lynds 981. Seen nearly pole-on, a jet driven by the young stellar object clears surrounding dust, producing a helical reflection nebula reminiscent of a snail's shell. T Tauri stars are the GCVS INT subtype of nebular variables, typified by irregular brightness variations driven by accretion, stellar flares, and circumstellar obscuration.
