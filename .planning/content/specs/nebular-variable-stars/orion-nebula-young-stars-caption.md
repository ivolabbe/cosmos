**Caption:** Three populations of young stars in the Orion Nebula as revealed by the VLT Survey Telescope. The Orion star-forming region gives its name to the "Orion variable" class of nebular variable stars (GCVS type IN), and contains numerous examples including T Tauri, FU Ori, and Herbig Ae/Be type objects.
