**Caption:** Plate depicting the constellation Orion from Johann Bayer's *Uranometria* (1661 edition), the landmark star atlas that introduced the systematic use of Greek letters to designate stars within each constellation. Bayer's alpha-through-omega scheme, first published in 1603, remains in use today.
