**Caption:** A high-resolution photograph of the visible solar spectrum, showing hundreds of dark Fraunhofer absorption lines crossing the rainbow of colours. Each dark line corresponds to a specific element in the Sun's atmosphere absorbing light at a characteristic wavelength — the method by which astronomers determine stellar chemical composition.
