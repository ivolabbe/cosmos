**Caption:** An illustration of emission and absorption spectra, demonstrating how astronomers analyse starlight. The top shows a rainbow-like continuous spectrum; the middle shows bright emission lines produced by hot gas; the bottom shows a continuous spectrum crossed by dark absorption lines — the signature of cooler gas in a stellar atmosphere absorbing specific wavelengths of the underlying starlight.
