**Caption:** A comparison of stellar spectra across the seven main spectral types, from hot blue O-type stars at top to cool red M-type stars at bottom. Each spectrum shows a distinct pattern of dark absorption lines, reflecting the star's surface temperature and the elements detectable at that temperature — the basis of stellar classification.
