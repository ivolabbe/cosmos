**Caption:** The colour of the sky from Gaia's Early Data Release 3 (December 2020), showing the total brightness and colour of 1.8 billion stars. Brighter regions represent denser concentrations of stars; the sweeping band of the Milky Way's disk is strikingly evident, with the bulge at centre. This is the modern culmination of the star-count tradition begun by Herschel.
