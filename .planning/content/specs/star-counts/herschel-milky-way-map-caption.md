**Caption:** William Herschel's 1785 drawing of the Milky Way, produced from his star-gauge counts in ~683 sky directions. The Sun is shown as a darker star near the centre of the flattened, irregular slab. Herschel assumed all stars were equally luminous, and did not account for interstellar dust — so the true galaxy turned out to be far larger and the Sun far from centre.
