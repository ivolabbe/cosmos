**Caption:** Radar images of asteroid 99942 Apophis (an Aten-class Earth-crossing asteroid, ~340 m across) obtained by NASA's Goldstone Solar System Radar on 26 March 2021. These data contributed to precise orbit determination that ruled out any impact risk from Apophis for at least 100 years. On 13 April 2029 Apophis will pass just ~32,000 km from Earth — closer than geostationary satellites.
