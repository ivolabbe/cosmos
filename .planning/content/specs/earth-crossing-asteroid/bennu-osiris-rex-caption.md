**Caption:** Mosaic image of asteroid 101955 Bennu (an Apollo-class Earth-crossing asteroid) assembled from 12 PolyCam images taken by NASA's OSIRIS-REx spacecraft on 2 December 2018. Bennu is ~490 m in mean diameter and has a cumulative impact probability of ~1 in 1,750 through 2300. OSIRIS-REx collected 121.6 g of surface sample from Bennu in October 2020, returning it to Earth in September 2023.
