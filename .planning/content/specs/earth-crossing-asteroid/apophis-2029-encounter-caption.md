**Caption:** Diagram showing the geometry of the Apophis close approach on 13 April 2029. Apophis will pass approximately 32,000 km from Earth's surface — within the ring of geostationary satellites (~35,786 km altitude). At magnitude ~3.1, it will be visible to the naked eye across Europe, Africa, and western Asia during this historic flyby.
