**Caption:** Ground-based image showing Caffau's star (SDSS J102915+172927) at the centre, a faint star in the constellation Leo. With [Fe/H] ≈ −5 and a mass below that of the Sun, this ancient ultra metal-poor star is probably more than 13 billion years old. Unusually, it shows neither strong carbon nor alpha-element enhancement, challenging standard models of early chemical enrichment.
