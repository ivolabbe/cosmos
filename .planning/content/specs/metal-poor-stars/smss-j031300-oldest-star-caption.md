**Caption:** Optical image of SMSS J031300.36−670839.3, discovered in 2014 using the SkyMapper Southern Sky Survey. This star holds the record for the lowest iron abundance of any known star, with [Fe/H] < −7.1 — more than ten million times lower in iron than the Sun. Its chemical pattern is consistent with enrichment by a single massive (~60 M☉) supernova in the early Universe.
