**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of Messier 92 (M92), a metal-poor globular cluster in the constellation Hercules. First discovered in 1777, this ancient stellar system contains ~330,000 stars and has an age close to that of the Universe (~13 billion years). As a metal-poor cluster, its stars formed in the early Galaxy before the interstellar medium was significantly enriched by heavy elements.
