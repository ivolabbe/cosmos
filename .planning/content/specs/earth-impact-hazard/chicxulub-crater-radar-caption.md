**Caption:** Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) image of the Yucatán Peninsula, Mexico, with the outline of the buried Chicxulub impact crater superimposed. The ~180–200 km crater, formed by a ~10 km impactor ~66 million years ago, is invisible at the surface but traceable through gravity anomalies and drill-core data. The impact triggered the Cretaceous–Paleogene mass extinction, wiping out ~75% of species including non-avian dinosaurs.
