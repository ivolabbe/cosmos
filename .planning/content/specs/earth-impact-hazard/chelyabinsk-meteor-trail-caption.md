**Caption:** The condensation trail left by the Chelyabinsk meteor on 15 February 2013 over Chelyabinsk Oblast, Russia. The 18-metre asteroid exploded at ~30 km altitude releasing 400–500 kilotonnes of TNT energy — roughly 30 times the Hiroshima bomb yield — injuring 1,491 people and damaging over 7,200 buildings, yet killing no one.
