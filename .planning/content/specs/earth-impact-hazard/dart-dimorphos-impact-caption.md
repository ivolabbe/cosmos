**Caption:** The asteroid Dimorphos as seen by DART's DRACO camera approximately 3 seconds before impact on 26 September 2022. The spacecraft was travelling at ~6.1 km/s when it struck the moonlet of asteroid Didymos, shortening Dimorphos's orbital period by 32 minutes — more than 25 times NASA's minimum success threshold. This was humanity's first successful demonstration of asteroid deflection.
