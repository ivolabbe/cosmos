**Caption:** Animated diagram of a Beta Lyrae-type (EB) eclipsing binary, showing two tidally distorted stars orbiting their common centre of mass. As each star periodically passes in front of the other, the total brightness of the system falls. The continuous variation in brightness — with no flat baseline — is characteristic of contact or semi-detached systems where the stars are gravitationally elongated.
