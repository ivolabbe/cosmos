**Caption:** Animated interferometric image of Algol (Beta Persei) captured by the CHARA array, showing the bright primary star Algol A being eclipsed by the dimmer subgiant Algol B over one 2.87-day orbital period. Resolution is approximately 200 times finer than the Hubble Space Telescope. This is the prototype EA-type eclipsing binary, first explained by John Goodricke in 1782.
