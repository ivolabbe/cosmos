**Caption:** Phase-folded light curve of the Algol-type eclipsing binary Zeta Phoenicis recorded by NASA's Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS). The deep primary minimum (cooler star eclipsing the hotter star) and shallower secondary minimum are clearly visible, along with the flat baseline between eclipses characteristic of EA-type (detached) systems.
