# fermions-vs-bosons — Image Caption

**Caption:** Diagram contrasting the quantum statistical behaviour of fermions and bosons. Fermions (left) obey the Pauli exclusion principle and occupy distinct quantum states; bosons (right) can accumulate without limit in the same state — the property that underlies lasers, superfluids, and Bose–Einstein condensates.
