# bose-einstein-condensate — Image Caption

**Caption:** Three false-colour snapshots showing the formation of a Bose–Einstein condensate as rubidium-87 atoms are cooled to below 170 billionths of a degree above absolute zero. The progression from red/yellow to blue/white marks increasing atomic density as atoms pile into the lowest quantum state — a direct consequence of Bose-Einstein statistics. This experiment at JILA (1995) was the first direct observation of a Bose–Einstein condensate.
