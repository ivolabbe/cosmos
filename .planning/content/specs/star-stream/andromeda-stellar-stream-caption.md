**Caption:** Individual stars resolved by the Hubble Space Telescope in part of the giant stellar stream surrounding the Andromeda Galaxy (M31). The stream is the tidal remnant of a companion galaxy torn apart by Andromeda's gravity — a visible example of galactic cannibalism.
