**Caption:** A giant, faint arc-loop stellar tidal stream in the halo of the nearby spiral galaxy Messier 63 (the Sunflower Galaxy). The stream extends approximately 29 kiloparsecs from the galaxy's centre and is the remnant of a disrupted dwarf satellite galaxy, likely cannibalized within the last 5 billion years.
