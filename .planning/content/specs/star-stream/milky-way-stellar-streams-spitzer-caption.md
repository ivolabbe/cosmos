**Caption:** An artist's annotated illustration based on Spitzer Space Telescope data showing three newly-discovered stellar streams arcing over the Milky Way Galaxy. The streams are remnants of globular clusters and cannibalized galaxies orbiting between 13,000 and 130,000 light-years from Earth.
