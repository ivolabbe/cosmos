**Caption:** Fragment A of the Antikythera Mechanism, an ancient Greek analogue computer (c. 150–100 BCE) housed in the National Archaeological Museum, Athens. Its gearing incorporated 223 lunar months — the Saros period — to predict future solar and lunar eclipses, demonstrating that the saros cycle was known to Greek astronomers more than two millennia ago.
