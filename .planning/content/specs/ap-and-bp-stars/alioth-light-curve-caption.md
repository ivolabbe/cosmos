**Caption:** TESS satellite light curve of Epsilon Ursae Majoris (Alioth), the brightest Ap star in the sky at apparent magnitude 1.77. The smooth rotational modulation visible in the light curve reflects the passage of chemical abundance patches across the stellar disk as the star rotates. Alioth's rotational period is approximately 5.1 days.
