**Caption:** TESS satellite light curve of Alpha2 Canum Venaticorum (Cor Caroli), the prototype Ap star and Alpha2 CVn variable. The periodic brightness variation over the star's 5.47-day rotation period results from chemical abundance spots on the stellar surface rotating in and out of view — a direct demonstration of the oblique rotator model. Typical amplitude is ~0.1 magnitudes in visible light.
