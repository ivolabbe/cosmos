**Caption:** Telescopic photograph of Cor Caroli (Alpha2 Canum Venaticorum), the prototype of the Alpha2 CVn class of rotationally variable Ap stars. The brighter component (Alpha2 CVn, spectral type A0pSiEuHg) is a classic Ap star with a magnetic field approximately 5,000 times stronger than Earth's, and its brightness varies between magnitudes 2.84 and 2.98 over a 5.47-day rotation period. Imaged through an Explorer 130P telescope in 2011.
