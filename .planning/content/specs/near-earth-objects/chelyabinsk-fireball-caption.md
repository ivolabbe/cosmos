**Caption:** The fireball from the Chelyabinsk superbolide streaks across the sky above Russia on February 15, 2013. The ~18 m asteroid released energy equivalent to 400–500 kilotonnes of TNT, injuring nearly 1,500 people from shattered glass and damaging over 7,200 buildings — yet caused no fatalities.
