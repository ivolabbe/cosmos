**File:** nasa-bh-accretion-disk.gif
**Caption:** Animated general-relativistic simulation of a black hole's warped accretion disk. As the disk orbits, relativistic Doppler beaming makes the approaching side brighter (left) and the receding side dimmer (right). Light from the disk below is lensed up and over the event horizon, producing the bright arc above. This simulation was created at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center using custom ray-tracing software.
