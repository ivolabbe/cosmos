**File:** ergosphere-diagram.svg
**Caption:** A diagram of a rotating Kerr black hole showing (from inside out) the inner event horizon, the outer event horizon, and the oblate ergosphere. Within the ergosphere, frame dragging forces all objects to co-rotate with the black hole, though escape is still possible. The ergosphere touches the outer horizon at the poles and extends furthest into space at the equator.
