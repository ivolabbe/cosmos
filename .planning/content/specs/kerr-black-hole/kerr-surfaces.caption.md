**File:** kerr-surfaces.svg
**Caption:** Cross-section of a Kerr black hole with spin parameter a = 0.99 (near-extremal), showing the outer ergosphere, outer event horizon, inner event horizon, and inner ergosphere. The ring singularity sits at the equatorial kink of the inner ergosphere. Compare with the spherically symmetric Schwarzschild case, which has a single horizon and no ergosphere.
