**Caption:** A NASA computer simulation of a black hole surrounded by a glowing accretion disk, with key features labeled: the event horizon, the photon ring (where light orbits), and the relativistically distorted disk. The extreme gravity of a Kerr black hole bends light from the back of the disk upward and over the top, creating the characteristic halo. Produced using general-relativistic ray-tracing at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
