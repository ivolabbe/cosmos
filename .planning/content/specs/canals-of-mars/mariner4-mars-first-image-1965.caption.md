The first close-up photograph of Mars, returned by NASA's Mariner 4 spacecraft on 14 July 1965. The image reveals a barren, Moon-like surface pocked with impact craters — no canals are visible. Mariner 4 returned 21 images showing 70 craters (5–120 km diameter) and measured a surface atmospheric pressure of only 4.1–7.0 millibars, ruling out liquid water at the surface.
