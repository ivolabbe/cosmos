**Caption:** Title page of Anders Jonas Ångström's landmark 1868 publication *Recherches sur le spectre solaire* (Research on the Solar Spectrum), co-authored with Robert Thalén. In this atlas Ångström mapped over 1,000 Fraunhofer absorption lines in the solar spectrum using wavelengths expressed in units of 10⁻¹⁰ metres — the scale that became known as the ångström.
