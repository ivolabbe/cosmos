**Caption:** False-colour Hubble Space Telescope image showing the blob structure within T Pyxidis's nova shell in greater detail. The false-colour processing reveals the irregular, clumpy distribution of ejected gas — a consequence of instabilities in the expanding shell and collisions between ejecta from multiple eruptions.
