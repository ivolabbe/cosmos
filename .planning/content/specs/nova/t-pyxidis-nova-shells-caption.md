**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of the recurrent nova T Pyxidis, revealing its expanding shell broken into more than 2,000 discrete gaseous blobs packed into a region 1 light-year across. The blobs, resembling shrapnel from an explosion, were produced by successive nova outbursts over the past century. T Pyxidis (T Pyx) has erupted six times since 1890.
