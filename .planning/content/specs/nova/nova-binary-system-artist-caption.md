**Caption:** Artist's concept showing the nova binary system HM Sagittae: a white dwarf (right) pulling hydrogen-rich material from its red giant companion (left) through gravitational attraction. The accreted material forms a blazing-hot accretion disk around the white dwarf, which can spontaneously ignite in a thermonuclear explosion — the nova outburst. HM Sge underwent a nova-like eruption in 1975 and has since been dimming; Hubble and other observatories continue monitoring it.
