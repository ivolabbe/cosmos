**Caption:** Radio image of the recurrent nova RS Ophiuchi in outburst, showing the expanding shell of ejected material from its 2006 eruption. RS Ophiuchi is a symbiotic recurrent nova involving a red giant and white dwarf; it has erupted at least seven times since 1898, most recently in August 2021.
