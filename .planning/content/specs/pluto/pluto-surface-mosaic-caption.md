**Caption:** The sharpest views of Pluto obtained by New Horizons during its 14 July 2015 flyby, assembled into a high-resolution mosaic. The scene spans roughly 80 km across and shows the remarkable variety of terrain — nitrogen-ice plains, water-ice mountains, and ancient cratered highlands.
