**Caption:** New Horizons' high-resolution farewell image of Pluto's receding crescent, taken on 14 July 2015 roughly 15 minutes after closest approach. Atmospheric haze layers are visible along the limb, scattering sunlight and producing a blue tinge — a discovery that surprised scientists expecting a simpler atmosphere.
