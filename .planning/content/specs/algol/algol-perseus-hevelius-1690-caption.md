**Caption:** The constellation Perseus as depicted in Johannes Hevelius's Uranographia (1690), showing Algol as a star in the severed head of the Gorgon Medusa. The illustration reflects the mythological tradition that gave Algol its association with the "Demon Star" — its position in the monster's eye, and the centuries-long awareness of its unusual, fluctuating character.
