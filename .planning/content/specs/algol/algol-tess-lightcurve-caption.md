**Caption:** Light curve of Algol recorded by NASA's Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS), showing the periodic brightness dips caused by the mutual eclipses of Aa1 and Aa2. The deep primary minima occur every 2.87 days when the cooler K-type subgiant transits the brighter B-type primary; shallower secondary minima are also visible midway between the primary dips.
