**Caption:** Animated interpolation of the orbit of Algol Aa2 around Algol Aa1, assembled from 55 images taken with the CHARA interferometer in the near-infrared H-band. One full orbit takes 2.87 days. The animation shows Aa2 (the cooler subgiant) completing a full revolution around the hotter, more massive Aa1.
