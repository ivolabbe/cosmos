**Caption:** The Algol triple star system as imaged on 12 August 2009 with the CHARA interferometer in the near-infrared H-band. The image has a resolution of approximately 0.5 milli-arcseconds. The round, blue-white disc is Algol Aa1 (the hot B8 main-sequence primary); the elongated, orange disc is Algol Aa2 (the cooler K0 subgiant secondary, tidally distorted by the primary's gravity). The smaller disc at lower left is the tertiary companion, Algol Ab.
