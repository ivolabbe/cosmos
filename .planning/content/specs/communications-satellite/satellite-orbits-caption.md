**Caption:** Diagram comparing the three main orbital regimes used by communications satellites: Low Earth Orbit (LEO, 160–2,000 km), Medium Earth Orbit (MEO, 2,000–36,000 km), and Geostationary Earth Orbit (GEO, 35,786 km). GEO satellites appear stationary from the ground and provide wide-area coverage but suffer from high latency (~480 ms round-trip). LEO constellations such as Starlink offer low latency (20–40 ms) but require hundreds to thousands of satellites for continuous global coverage.
