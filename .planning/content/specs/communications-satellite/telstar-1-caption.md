**Caption:** Telstar 1, the first communications satellite to relay live transatlantic television signals, launched July 10, 1962. Built by Bell Telephone Laboratories, Telstar 1 occupied a medium-altitude elliptical orbit and on July 23, 1962 transmitted the first publicly available live television signal across the Atlantic. Although not geostationary, it demonstrated the practical potential of satellite communications and inspired the famous pop song of the same name.
