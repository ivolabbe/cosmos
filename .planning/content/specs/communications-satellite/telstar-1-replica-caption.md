**Caption:** Replica of Telstar 1, the pioneering communications satellite that relayed the first live transatlantic television broadcast in 1962, on display at the Science Museum in London. The spherical body studded with solar cells and antennas was characteristic of early satellite design. Telstar 1 was built by Bell Telephone Laboratories and launched on a Thor-Delta rocket from Cape Canaveral on July 10, 1962.
