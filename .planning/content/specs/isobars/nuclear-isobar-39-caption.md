**Caption:** Mass parabola for the A = 39 isobar chain, plotting exact atomic mass against atomic number Z. The parabolic curve arises from the Coulomb and asymmetry terms in the Bethe–Weizsäcker semi-empirical mass formula. The minimum of the parabola corresponds to the most stable isobar; odd-A chains like A = 39 have a single parabola with one stable endpoint (³⁹K, Z = 19).
