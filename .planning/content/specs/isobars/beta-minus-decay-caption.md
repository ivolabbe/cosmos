**Caption:** Schematic of beta-minus decay: a neutron (n⁰) in the nucleus converts to a proton (p⁺), emitting an electron (e⁻) and an electron antineutrino (ν̄ₑ). The mass number A is unchanged by this process, so the parent and daughter nuclei are isobars — they share the same A but differ in atomic number Z by one unit.
