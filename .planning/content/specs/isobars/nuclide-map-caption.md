**Caption:** Chart of all known nuclides plotted by neutron number (x-axis) and proton number Z (y-axis), coloured by dominant decay mode (black = stable, orange = beta-minus, blue = beta-plus/electron capture, etc.). Isobars lie along diagonal lines of constant mass number A = Z + N running from upper-left to lower-right. The stable valley (black squares) shows how beta decay drives unstable nuclides toward the most stable isobar for each A.
