**Caption:** ESO's Very Large Telescope (VLT) at Cerro Paranal, Chile, showing all four 8.2-metre Unit Telescopes against the dark skies of the Atacama Desert. The VLT is the world's most productive ground-based optical observatory, capable of operating as four independent telescopes or combining their light as an interferometer.
