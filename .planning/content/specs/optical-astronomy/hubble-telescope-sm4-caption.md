**Caption:** The Hubble Space Telescope captured by Space Shuttle Atlantis's robotic arm during Servicing Mission 4 in May 2009. Hubble, launched in 1990, observes in near-ultraviolet, visible, and near-infrared light above Earth's atmosphere, providing diffraction-limited images unaffected by atmospheric seeing.
