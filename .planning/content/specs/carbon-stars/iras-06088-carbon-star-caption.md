**IRAS 06088+1909 — near-infrared 2MASS image** — an infrared view of an evolved carbon star illustrating the extreme redness that results from a thick circumstellar envelope of carbon-rich dust. In the near-infrared, where 2MASS observed, the dust emission from amorphous carbon and silicon carbide (SiC) grains peaks, making carbon stars among the most luminous infrared sources in the sky. The faint diffraction artefacts around bright stars are filter-glint effects from the detector array, not physical features.
