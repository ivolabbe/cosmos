**Caption:** Neptune's largest moon Triton, imaged by Voyager 2 in 1989. Triton orbits Neptune in a retrograde direction — opposite to the planet's rotation — strong evidence that it was captured from the outer Solar System rather than forming alongside Neptune. Its composition and size are consistent with a large Kuiper Belt object, and its capture likely occurred via binary dissociation when a KBO binary pair encountered Neptune early in solar system history.
