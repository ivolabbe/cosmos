**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope images of the galaxy NGC 4993 showing the optical counterpart (kilonova SSS17a) of the neutron star merger GW170817, detected on August 17, 2017. The kilonova's fading glow is visible as a point source near the galaxy's centre in the left panel and absent in the earlier archival image at right.
