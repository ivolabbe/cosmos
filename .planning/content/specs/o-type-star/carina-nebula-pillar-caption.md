**Caption:** A towering gas pillar in the Carina Nebula, located 7,500 light-years away in the constellation Carina. The pillar's irregular top has been eroded by intense ultraviolet radiation and stellar winds from nearby O-type stars, including those in the Trumpler 14 and Trumpler 16 clusters that dominate the nebula. At the pillar's tip, new stars are being triggered into formation by the same radiation pressure that is slowly destroying the structure.
