**Caption:** Close-up of the Tarantula Nebula (30 Doradus) in the Large Magellanic Cloud, the most intensely star-forming region in the Local Group. This vast ionized hydrogen nebula is powered almost entirely by massive O-type stars and Wolf–Rayet stars clustered in R136, including some of the most luminous and hottest stars known. The nebula is so bright that, if placed at the distance of the Orion Nebula, it would cast shadows on Earth.
