**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope's sharpest view of the Orion Nebula interior, revealing more than 3,000 young stars in a landscape of dust and gas. At the centre lies the Trapezium — four massive O-type stars whose ultraviolet radiation ionizes the entire surrounding nebula and sculpts the cavern of glowing gas. The nebula lies about 1,500 light-years from Earth and is the nearest site of ongoing high-mass star formation.
