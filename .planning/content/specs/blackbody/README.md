## Notes for Writer

1. **Planck constant (h) vs. Boltzmann constant (k_B):**
   - Planck: 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s (relates energy to frequency, E = hν)
   - Boltzmann: 1.381 × 10⁻²³ J/K (appears in exponent of Planck function)

2. **Temperature references:**
   - CMB: 2.725 K (coldest natural blackbody)
   - Sun surface: 5,778 K (good relatable example)
   - Tungsten filament: 2,500 K (everyday example)

3. **Key equations (conceptual — no math needed):**
   - Wien: λ_peak = b/T (inverse relationship)
   - Stefan-Boltzmann: P = σAT⁴ (fourth-power dependence)
   - Planck: Describes full spectrum distribution

4. **Historical arc:** Kirchhoff (1860) → Wien (1893) → Stefan-Boltzmann (1879) → Planck (1900) → CMB discovery (1964) → COBE/FIRAS (1989–2006)

5. **Why it matters:** Planck's solution to blackbody problem invented quantum mechanics and revolutionized physics.
