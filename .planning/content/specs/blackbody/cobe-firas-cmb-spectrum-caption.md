**Caption:** The cosmic microwave background spectrum measured by the COBE satellite's FIRAS instrument, showing radiation intensity versus frequency (in waves per centimeter). The data points (with error bars) match a perfect blackbody curve at temperature 2.725 K so precisely that the theoretical curve is indistinguishable from the observations—agreement better than 1 part in 10,000. This is the most perfect blackbody spectrum ever measured in nature, confirming the Big Bang model and demonstrating that the CMB originates from thermal equilibrium in the early universe.
