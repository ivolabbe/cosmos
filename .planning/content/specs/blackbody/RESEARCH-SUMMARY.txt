KEY FINDINGS & VERIFIED DATA
================================================================================

FUNDAMENTAL LAWS:

1. Planck's Law (October 19, 1900)
   - Describes spectral density of blackbody radiation
   - First use of energy quantization (quanta)
   - Planck constant h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s
   - Solved "ultraviolet catastrophe" of classical physics
   - Launched quantum mechanics

2. Wien's Displacement Law (1893)
   - λ_peak = b / T
   - Wien constant b = 2.898 × 10⁻³ m·K
   - Peak wavelength inversely proportional to temperature
   - Examples: Sun (5,778 K) peaks at ~500 nm (green)
              Betelgeuse (3,800 K) mostly infrared

3. Stefan-Boltzmann Law
   - P = σAT⁴
   - Stefan-Boltzmann constant σ = 5.670374 × 10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴)
   - Total power ∝ T⁴
   - At 300 K, 1 m² blackbody emits ~460 W

4. Kirchhoff's Law (1860)
   - At thermal equilibrium: Power radiated = Power absorbed
   - Universal function for blackbody emissivity
   - Laid groundwork for Planck's solution

ASTROPHYSICAL APPLICATIONS:

- Stars approximate blackbodies (effective temperature from color/spectrum)
- Hertzsprung-Russell diagram uses Stefan-Boltzmann law
- Wien's law enables stellar temperature estimation

COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND:

- Temperature: 2.72548 ± 0.00057 K (COBE/FIRAS measurement)
- Most perfect blackbody spectrum ever measured
- Agrees with perfect blackbody to better than 1 part in 10,000
- Discovery: Penzias & Wilson (1964) — Nobel Prize 2006
- COBE/FIRAS measurements: 1989–1993
- CMB monopole confirms Big Bang model
- Minor anisotropies (1 part in 100,000) contain early universe information
