**Caption:** Planck's blackbody radiation spectrum at 5800 K (approximately the Sun's surface temperature), showing the distribution of radiation intensity across frequency (normalized units). The curve demonstrates peak intensity in the visible and near-infrared region, and contrasts Planck's law (solid curve) with classical predictions (dotted lines: Wien's law, Rayleigh-Jeans law). The graph illustrates why classical physics failed at high frequencies and why Planck's quantum hypothesis was essential.
