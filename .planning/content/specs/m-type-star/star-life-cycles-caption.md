**Caption:** Diagram comparing the life cycles of low-mass stars (left, including M-type red dwarfs) and high-mass stars (right). Red dwarfs have extraordinarily long lifetimes — a 0.1 solar-mass M dwarf can fuse hydrogen for approximately 10 trillion years, far exceeding the current age of the universe.
