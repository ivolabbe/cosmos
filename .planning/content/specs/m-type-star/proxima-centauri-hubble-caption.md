**Caption:** False-colour Hubble Space Telescope image of Proxima Centauri, the nearest star to the Sun at 4.25 light-years distance. A prototypical late M-type dwarf (spectral type M5.5Ve), Proxima Centauri has a mass of only 12% that of the Sun and is too faint to see with the naked eye despite its proximity. The diffraction spikes are an artefact of the telescope's optics.
