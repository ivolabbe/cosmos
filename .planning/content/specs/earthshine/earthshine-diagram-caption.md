# Caption: earthshine-diagram.svg

**Caption:** Diagram illustrating the geometry of earthshine: sunlight (yellow) strikes Earth, is reflected onto the Moon's dark face, and a small fraction returns to an observer on Earth. The phenomenon is visible because Earth presents a nearly full phase when the Moon is a thin crescent.
