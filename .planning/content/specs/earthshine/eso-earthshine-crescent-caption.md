# Caption: eso-earthshine-crescent.jpg

**Caption:** A thin crescent Moon glowing with earthshine, imaged by the European Southern Observatory. The faint rainbow-like colour gradient across the dark portion results from Earth's reflected light passing through the lunar atmosphere before returning to us — the same light astronomers analyse to search for biosignatures as if Earth were a distant exoplanet.
