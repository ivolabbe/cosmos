**Caption:** Global map of the Moon's gravity field as measured by NASA's GRAIL mission (Mercator projection). The far side of the Moon is shown at centre; the near side (as seen from Earth) appears at either edge. Red regions correspond to mass excesses producing higher local gravity; blue regions correspond to mass deficits producing lower gravity. Values are in milliGalileos (1 Galileo = 1 cm/s²).
