**Caption:** Topographic map of the Moon showing lunar elevation data from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter with GRAIL gravity gradient anomalies bordering the Oceanus Procellarum region overlaid in blue. These borders outline ancient lava-flooded rift zones beneath the lunar surface, illustrating the relationship between mascon-related gravity anomalies and volcanic features.
