**Caption:** Maps of the Moon showing Bouguer gravity anomalies as measured by NASA's GRAIL mission. Red areas indicate stronger gravity (mass excesses/mascons), while blue areas indicate weaker gravity (mass deficits). The circular red concentrations over the large impact basins are the mascons.
