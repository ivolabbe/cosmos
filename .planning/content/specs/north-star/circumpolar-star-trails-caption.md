**Caption:** A 30-minute long-exposure photograph centred on Polaris, showing star trails as arcs curving around the nearly-stationary North Star. Stars of different colours — blue, white, yellow, and red — reflect their spectral types. The near-absence of trailing in Polaris itself demonstrates why it has served as a navigation reference for millennia.
