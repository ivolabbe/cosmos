**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image revealing the Polaris triple star system. The bright primary (Polaris Aa) is a yellow supergiant Cepheid variable; Polaris B, the outer companion, is visible as a fainter star to the lower left. The inner companion Polaris Ab is not individually resolved here.
