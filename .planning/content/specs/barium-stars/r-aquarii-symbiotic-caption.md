**Caption:** Hubble Space Telescope image of R Aquarii, a symbiotic binary system consisting of a pulsating Mira variable giant star and a white dwarf companion. Material ejected from the giant forms a complex nebula around the pair. Symbiotic binary systems share the essential binary architecture of barium stars — a giant star transferring mass to a white dwarf companion — making this a vivid illustration of the mass-transfer processes that create barium star chemical peculiarities.
