**Caption:** Diagram showing the Roche lobes of a detached binary star system with equal-mass components (mass ratio q=1). Each lobe defines the region of space within which material is gravitationally bound to that star. When the larger (AGB) star expands to fill its Roche lobe, it transfers mass through the inner Lagrangian point (L1) to its companion — one of the key mechanisms by which barium stars acquire their s-process enriched material.
