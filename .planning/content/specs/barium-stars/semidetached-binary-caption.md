**Caption:** Diagram of a semidetached binary system (mass ratio q=3) in which the larger star has expanded to fill its Roche lobe (indicated by the black equipotential boundary). Material flows through the inner Lagrangian point toward the smaller companion. This geometry illustrates the Roche lobe overflow scenario that operates in some barium star systems, where the AGB progenitor transfers s-process enriched material to the companion that will become the observed barium star.
