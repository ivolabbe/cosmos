**Caption:** Supernova SN 1997ff, the farthest Type Ia supernova ever observed at the time of its discovery. Located 10 billion light-years away (redshift z~1.7) in an elliptical galaxy, this supernova was among the key observations that led to the discovery of cosmic acceleration. The bright point in the center is the supernova explosion; the surrounding galaxy provides the host system.
