**Caption:** The full GHOST spectrograph mosaic of HD 222925, a remarkably bright, chemically complex Ap star. This mosaic displays the star's light across wavelengths from approximately 350 to 1,015 nm; the dark absorption lines are signatures of 65 chemical elements detected in the stellar atmosphere — the widest elemental census ever made of a star other than the Sun.
