**Caption:** Sigma Draconis (officially named Alsafi), a K0 V star at 18.8 light-years from Earth. Sigma Draconis is the standard MK spectral anchor for the K0 V subtype — the hottest and most Sun-like end of the orange dwarf sequence. It is a prime example of the "Goldilocks star" class: a stable, long-lived K dwarf with a quiet stellar environment conducive to planetary habitability.
