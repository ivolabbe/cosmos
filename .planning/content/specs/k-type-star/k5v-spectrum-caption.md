**Caption:** Normalised spectrum of a K5V (orange dwarf) star from 2000 to 10000 Ångströms, based on ESO library data. The spectrum shows the characteristic features of K-type stars: very weak hydrogen Balmer absorption, numerous neutral metal absorption lines (Fe I, Mn I, Si I), and the beginnings of molecular bands (CN, OH) that become prominent in cooler M-type stars. The orange colour of K dwarfs reflects their peak emission in the red-visible range.
