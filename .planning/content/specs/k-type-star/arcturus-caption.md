**Caption:** Optical observation of Arcturus (Alpha Boötis), a K1.5 III giant and the fourth-brightest star in the night sky. At a distance of 36.7 light-years, Arcturus has a surface temperature of ~4,286 K and a luminosity ~170 times the Sun's — typical of an evolved K-type giant. Its distinctly orange colour is immediately apparent, making it one of the most recognisable naked-eye examples of a K-type star.
