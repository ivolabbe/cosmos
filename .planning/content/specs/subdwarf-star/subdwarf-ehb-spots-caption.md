**Caption:** Artist's impression comparing stellar spots on an extreme horizontal branch (hot subdwarf) star with sunspots. Unlike the darker, cooler sunspots on our Sun, spots on these hot compact stars are brighter and hotter than the surrounding stellar surface, and can cover up to a quarter of the star's face.
