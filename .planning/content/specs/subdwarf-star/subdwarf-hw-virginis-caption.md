**Caption:** Computer visualisation of HW Virginis, an eclipsing binary system in which a hot subdwarf B star orbits with a red dwarf companion. The system has an orbital period of about 2.8 hours and is one of the best-studied sdB binaries, illustrating the close-binary formation channel for hot subdwarfs.
